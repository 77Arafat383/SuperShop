🚀 Exciting Project Update: **SuperShop — Modern Sales & Inventory Tracking System!** 🛒⚡

Managing retail inventory, point-of-sale transactions, and supplier ledgers can quickly become complex. To tackle this, we built **SuperShop (RBMS)** — a full-stack, enterprise-grade inventory and POS management platform designed for speed, security, and seamless workflow automation!

📹 **Check out the video demo below!** 👇

---

### 💡 **Key Features & Achievements:**
- ⚡ **Ultra-Fast POS Terminal**: Real-time barcode laser simulation, sub-50ms responsive interactions, and instant 80mm thermal receipt / A4 invoice generation.
- 💳 **Flexible Payment Integration**: Native support for Cash, bKash, Nagad, Card, and Split Payments.
- 🔐 **Multi-Role RBAC**: 4 distinct access levels (Admin, Inventory Manager, Purchase Manager, Cashier) with approval & promotion workflows.
- 📦 **Inventory & Purchase Orders**: Automated low-stock alerts, barcode label generator, supplier due ledgers, and payment history receipts.
- 📊 **Executive Analytics**: Real-time sales trends, Gross Profit, COGS calculation, and inventory valuation charts.

---

### 🛠️ **Tech Stack:**
- **Frontend / Framework:** Next.js 14 (App Router), React 18, TypeScript
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS & Lucide Icons
- **Database:** Supabase (PostgreSQL)
- **Deployment & Hosting:** Vercel (CI/CD Pipeline & Serverless Functions)
- **Analytics & Data Viz:** Recharts & Canvas-Confetti

Building this platform was an incredible experience in crafting optimized database queries, fast interactive UI components, and real-time state management.
